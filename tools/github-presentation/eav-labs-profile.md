**EAV Labs** is my personal engineering lab — a place to design, build, test, document, and ship practical software across web, mobile, backend, and business workflows.

Each project starts with a real operational problem and is developed as a software product, with attention to architecture, implementation, testing, documentation, containerized development, CI/CD, and deployment readiness.

## Featured systems

| | System | Focus | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **01** | [**EAV Insight**](https://github.com/eav-labs-dev/eav-insight-api) | Document intake, operational reporting, searchable records | FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Docker |

## Engineering approach

**Design real workflows · Build production-style systems · Document clearly · Test deliberately · Deploy responsibly**

The repositories are intentionally varied in stack and product surface. Together they show how I approach software engineering across APIs, mobile workflows, persistence, business logic, automation, delivery, and maintainability.
